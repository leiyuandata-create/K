\ppline{1}{A is $(x,\ d-kx^{2})$, $x>0$. Wall height $d-kx^{2}$; triangle height $d-(d-kx^{2})=kx^{2}$}
\ppline{2}{Rectangle $=2x(d-kx^{2})=2dx-2kx^{3}$;\ \ triangle $=x\cdot kx^{2}=kx^{3}$}
\ppline{3}{Area $A=2dx-2kx^{3}+kx^{3}=2dx-kx^{3}$}
\pplineT{4}{$A'=2d-3kx^{2}=0$, so $kx^{2}=\dfrac{2d}{3}$}
\ppline{5}{$A''=-6kx<0$ (as $k,x>0$), so this is a maximum}
\pplineT{6}{At the maximum: rectangle $=2x(d-kx^{2})=2x\Bigl(d-\dfrac{2d}{3}\Bigr)=\dfrac{2dx}{3}$}
\pplineT{7}{Triangle $=x\cdot kx^{2}=x\cdot\dfrac{2d}{3}=\dfrac{2dx}{3}$}
\ppline{8}{So the rectangle and the triangle have equal areas at the maximum.}
